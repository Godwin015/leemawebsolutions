// Leadership & Expertise

Edward Godwin

Founder & Chief Strategy Officer

A specialist in Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence with a deep background in vulnerability assessment and scalable application architecture. Godwin leads the technical vision for Leema Web Solutions, ensuring every project meets elite security standards.

[ Professional Journey ]

His expertise in Artificial Intelligence is built on a rigorous academic foundation, culminating in a Higher National Diploma in Artificial Intelligence Technology from Abdu Gusau Polytechnic. This focus has allowed him to lead innovative projects, such as the development and deployment of a Speech-to-text transcription system leveraging advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP). His approach to AI is rooted in practical application, moving beyond theory to build intelligent tools that solve real-world efficiency challenges.

In the realm of Web Development, he has served as a remote Website Administrator for several organizations, ranging from Private organizations, Government organization and Non-governmental Organization. He specializes in building fully functional, scalable websites using the Django framework & Python, Laravel framework & PHP, often integrating complex features like QR code-based payment portals to optimize user experience and operational security.

His Cybersecurity journey involves deep technical training and offensive security researches. Through intensive internships at Cyber Secured India, Prodigy Infotech and Halogen Academy, he is proficient in penetration testing, digital forensics, and network scanning using industry-standard tools like Nmap, Burp Suite, and Metasploit. Beyond assessments, He has developed custom security software, including highly effective encryption tools, packet sniffers, and keyloggers, ensuring that he understands threats from both a defensive and offensive perspective.
